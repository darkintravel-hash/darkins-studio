Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

How do I book a session?

Choose a package above and hit "Enquire Now" — it opens a pre-filled email so you can send your preferred date and location straight away.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a 30% non-refundable deposit secures your date. The balance is due on or before the day of the shoot.

How long until I receive my photos or video?

Turnaround varies by package — anywhere from 3 to 10 days for photography, and 5 to 10 days for video. Rush delivery is available as an add-on.

Do you travel outside your local area?

Yes. Local sessions are included in package pricing; travel further out may incur an additional fee depending on distance.

Can I customise a package?

Absolutely — get in touch and we'll tailor coverage, hours, or deliverables to fit exactly what you need.
